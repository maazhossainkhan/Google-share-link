Google does not offer a public API for creating share.google links. The link must come from Google's own app.

  1. Copy the Canvau short link you just created.
  2. Open the page in the Google app on Android, then tap its Share option.
  3. Copy the generated share.google or search.app URL and paste it into that link's card below.
Canvau will not invent Google tokens or mark a link as converted until a Google-domain URL is supplied.
